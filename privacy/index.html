Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Short version: Everything you log stays on your device. The only thing that ever leaves it is a suggestion you choose to send, and only when you tap Send.

What Dialed stores

Your brews, coffees, recipes, equipment and preferences are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's local database. They are not sent to us or to any third party. If you use iCloud device backups, this data is included in your backup under Apple's terms.

What Dialed does not do

Dialed has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. We do not collect, sell or share personal information.

The coffee assistant

The assistant runs entirely on your device. When available it uses Apple's on-device foundation model (Apple Intelligence); your questions and brew history are processed locally and never leave your iPhone.

Purchases

Dialed Pro is sold through the App Store. Payments are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details. The app only asks the App Store whether you own Pro.

Suggestions from supporters

If you've bought Dialed Pro, you can send the developer an idea or a bug report from inside the app. This only happens when you write a message and tap Send. What's sent: your message, the type (idea, bug or other), an email address only if you choose to add one, the app version, your iPhone model, iOS version and language, and Apple's signed proof of your Pro purchase.

The purchase proof is only used to check that the message comes from a supporter and to limit how many can be sent per day; we store a one-way scrambled form of its ID, not the ID itself, and never see your Apple ID or payment details. Nothing from your journal is sent. Messages are stored in Google Firebase (European region) and the developer gets a notification of each one. They're used only to improve Dialed and, if you left an email, to reply to you. To have a message deleted, email the address below.

Children

Dialed does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
