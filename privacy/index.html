Dialed

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Short version: Your brews, coffees and notes stay on your phone. Dialed sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports (which features are used, app performance and crashes) to Google Firebase so we can improve it. You can turn this off in Settings.

What Dialed stores

Your brews, coffees, recipes, equipment and preferences are stored only on your phone, in the app's local database. They are not sent to us or to any third party. If you use iCloud device backups (iPhone) or Google backup (Android), this data is included in your backup under Apple's or Google's terms.

What Dialed does not do

Dialed has no account system, no advertising and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites. It does not collect your advertising identifier and never sells or shares personal information.

Usage statistics and crash reports

To learn which features help people and whether the app is fast and stable, Dialed uses two Google services: Google Analytics for Firebase (usage statistics) and Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports). Google processes this data on our behalf under the Firebase data processing terms. Advertising features are turned off: no advertising identifier is collected and the data isn't used for ads or ad personalization.

What is sent: the names of events (for example "brew logged", "suggestion shown", "share opened", "paywall viewed"), the app version, operating system version, device model and language, your approximate region (country or city, which Google derives from your IP address; the IP address itself isn't stored), coarse settings (such as whether taste tracking is on or whether you have Pro), rounded or bucketed counts and timings (for example "6–20 brews" or "under 10 seconds to log"), app performance information, crash reports about the app's own code (where in the code it crashed and the last few screens or actions before it), and a random app-instance identifier created by Firebase so the same install is counted once. That identifier is reset if you delete the app and isn't linked to your name, email or any account.

What is never sent: your journal content, coffee names, roasters, notes, grind settings or recipes, the questions you ask the assistant, your precise location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifier.

Retention: usage events are kept for up to 14 months and then only in aggregate; crash reports are kept for 90 days. Because the data isn't linked to your identity, we can't look up one person's statistics, but you can reset them by deleting and reinstalling the app.

Turning it off: Settings → About → "Share usage and crash data". When it's off, Dialed sends no usage statistics and no crash reports at all. Nothing is ever sent from sample data or test builds.

The coffee assistant

The assistant runs entirely on your device. When available it uses the phone's own on-device model (Apple Intelligence on iPhone, Gemini Nano on supported Android phones); your questions and brew history are processed locally and never leave your phone.

Purchases

Dialed Pro is sold through the App Store on iPhone and Google Play on Android. Payments are processed by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details. The app only asks the store whether you own Pro.

Suggestions from supporters

If you've bought Dialed Pro, you can send the developer an idea or a bug report from inside the app. This only happens when you write a message and tap Send. What's sent: your message, the type (idea, bug or other), an email address only if you choose to add one, the app version, your phone model, OS version and language, and the store's signed proof of your Pro purchase.

The purchase proof is only used to check that the message comes from a supporter and to limit how many can be sent per day; we store a one-way scrambled form of its ID, not the ID itself, and never see your Apple or Google account or payment details. Nothing from your journal is sent. Messages are stored in Google Firebase (European region) and the developer gets a notification of each one. They're used only to improve Dialed and, if you left an email, to reply to you. To have a message deleted, email the address below.

Children

Dialed does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes
