Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Short version: Dialed does not collect any data. Everything you log stays on your device.

What Dialed stores

Your brews, coffees, recipes, equipment and preferences are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's local database. They are not sent to us or to any third party. If you use iCloud device backups, this data is included in your backup under Apple's terms.

What Dialed does not do

Dialed has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. We do not collect, sell or share personal information.

The coffee assistant

The assistant runs entirely on your device. When available it uses Apple's on-device foundation model (Apple Intelligence); your questions and brew history are processed locally and never leave your iPhone.

Purchases

Dialed Pro is sold through the App Store. Payments are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details. The app only asks the App Store whether you own Pro.

Children

Dialed does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
